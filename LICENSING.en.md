A mixed purpose is assessed by its actual primary purpose. This software explanation does not change the CC definition of NonCommercial for documentation.
- Internal software use means use by the same legal entity's employees/officers on its behalf and under its control. Affiliates, independent contractors and customers are separate recipients; an NDA alone does not make use internal. Providing functionality remotely differs from delivering a code copy, but commercial SaaS still requires permission even without distribution. The default modified-software grant is for private noncommercial use.

General recipients do not automatically receive those additional rights. A brand name alone does not identify the contracting parties; the actual grant must identify parties and subject matter.
- Independent rights under hosting-platform terms, including GitHub's terms, are not revoked by repository restrictions or contribution procedures. Viewing/forking a public repository is not described as permissible only for official contributions. Applicable account/service terms must be checked separately.
- These terms do not prohibit statutory copyright exceptions, independent implementations of unprotected ideas/methods, or previously valid permissions.
- For a use outside available permissions, obtain a separate written agreement from a party authorized to grant the required rights. Contact **mail@401spag.com**.

## Relationship between documents

The English `LICENSE-CODE` governs the covered software grant and the existing-software contribution exception. `LICENSING.md` identifies material scope. Summaries in the README, this guide and CONTRIBUTING do not expand or narrow the operative software grant. Documentation follows the unmodified CC legal text and separately granted documentation contribution permissions.

Additional integration/sublicensing rights in a new contribution depend on the expressly accepted agreement identifying its subject, parties and version. A separate contract applies only to the rights, material and parties it identifies. These statements do not create a blanket priority rule that excludes independent earlier permissions or statutory exceptions.

Preserve the text and covered commit at the time of agreement; later edits do not automatically change earlier agreements.
